The NeiMozhi Legacy

Crafted in the Heart of Uthukuli

For generations, the unique soil, climate, and water of Uthukuli have yielded milk of unparalleled quality. We translate this heritage into **NeiMozhi** ('The Language of Ghee').

Our products are crafted inside a traditional family kitchen. We do not mass-produce. We prepare each batch only after an order is received, ensuring that the incredible, nutty aroma of slow-melted butter stays locked inside our glass jars until they reach your kitchen.
